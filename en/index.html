About Us

A space to train and learn

At GRIP Climbing Club we believe climbing is more than a sport: it's discipline, community, and adventure. That's why we built a gym for every level — from someone touching a hold for the first time to experienced climbers chasing their next project.

It doesn't matter if you're from Calarcá, Armenia, Salento or just passing through the Coffee Region. Everyone is welcome in our community.

  • ✦ Routes refreshed regularly for every level.
  • ✦ Experienced instructors and a welcoming community.
  • ✦ Inspected safety equipment available.
  • ✦ Perfect spot to train after work or class.

No need to know anything about climbing. Just bring the energy — we'll handle the rest.

  1. 1

    Message us on Instagram

    Send a DM to @murodeescaladagrip to reserve your spot and ask any questions.

  2. 2

    Come comfortable

    Bring comfortable clothes (sweatpants, leggings, shorts) and socks. We provide climbing shoes and all safety gear.

    Initial briefing

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from people climbing at GRIP for the first time.

Do I need previous climbing experience?

No. GRIP Climbing Club is designed for all levels. If it's your first time, our instructors will teach you safety techniques and basic movement before you go on the wall.

What should I bring on my first visit?

Just comfortable clothes that allow you to move (sweatpants, leggings or shorts) and a pair of socks. We provide climbing shoes and all safety equipment.

What is the minimum age?

We welcome climbers from age 6 and up. For minors, we require guardian authorization. We have special programs for kids and schools.

Do I need to book or can I just walk in?

You can walk in during opening hours. If you're coming as a group, for the first time, or want a class, we recommend messaging us on Instagram to secure your spot.

How long does a climbing session last?

A typical session lasts 1.5 to 2 hours. That's enough time to warm up, climb several routes and rest between attempts without getting exhausted.

Is it far from Armenia and other towns?

Calarcá is just 10 minutes from Armenia and well-connected to the rest of Quindío. We welcome climbers from Armenia, Salento, Montenegro, La Tebaida, Circasia, Quimbaya, Filandia and the entire Coffee Region.

Can I come from another city in Colombia?

Absolutely. We welcome climbers from all over Colombia — Bogotá, Medellín, Cali, Pereira, Manizales, Ibagué and beyond. Calarcá is 4–5 hours by road from Bogotá, 5–6 hours from Medellín, 4 hours from Cali, and just 1 hour from Pereira International Airport. If you're planning a trip to the Coffee Region, adding a climbing session at GRIP is an unforgettable experience.

Do you offer day passes for visitors and tourists?

Yes. The day pass is perfect for anyone visiting Quindío who wants to climb without a long-term commitment. It includes full wall access, safety equipment and initial guidance — ideal for traveling climbers or tourists wanting to try climbing in the heart of the Coffee Region.

Is indoor climbing dangerous?

Indoor climbing is very safe when protocols are followed. At GRIP we use high-density mats for bouldering, certified ropes for top-rope, and equipment is regularly inspected. Instructors supervise beginners.

Do you have English-speaking staff?

Yes — we can help travelers and tourists in English. Just message us beforehand on Instagram and we'll make sure you have a great experience.
